# Grandma's Famous Lasagna

## Overview
Prepare the legendary family lasagna recipe for Sunday dinner. Serves 8-10 hungry relatives. The dish should be ready by 2 PM and must include the secret ingredient (extra love). Leftovers will be distributed in Tupperware containers.

More info and preparation details can be found in `grandma's-secret-notebook.pdf`

Technical Details
Italian cuisine (Nonna style), 9x13 baking dish, 375°F oven. Layers are assembled via the "don't skimp on cheese" method. Ingredients are accessed via `Pantry.check(item)` or extension trips to the grocery store for missing items.

## Tasks

### Phase 1: Sauce Preparation
- [ ] Brown 1.5 lbs ground beef with onions
- [ ] Add 2 cans crushed tomatoes and herbs
- [ ] Simmer for at least 45 minutes
- [ ] Taste and adjust seasoning (more garlic never hurts)

### Phase 2: Cheese Mixture
- [ ] Mix 2 lbs ricotta with 2 beaten eggs
- [ ] Add 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- [ ] Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg
- [ ] Shred 1 lb mozzarella and grate 1 cup parmesan

### Phase 3: Assembly — The Layering
- [ ] Spread thin layer of sauce on bottom of dish
- [ ] Place first layer of noodles (no overlap!)
- [ ] Add ricotta mixture, then sauce, then mozzarella
- [ ] Repeat layers 3 times, end with sauce and parmesan on top

### Phase 4: Baking & Serving
- [ ] Cover with foil and bake 45 minutes at 375°F
- [ ] Remove foil and bake additional 15 minutes until bubbly
- [ ] Rest for 10 minutes before cutting (CRITICAL!)

### Localization
